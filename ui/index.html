Help & FAQ

How do I connect for the first time?

Put the phone on the same Wi-Fi as the TV and turn the TV on. Open ⚙, tap your TV in "TVs found on your Wi-Fi" (or type its IP from the TV's Settings › Network), then Save. A 6-character code appears on the TV. Type it into the phone. You only do this once.

The TV isn't found in the list

Make sure the TV is on, not in standby, and both are on the same Wi-Fi (not a guest network). You can always type the TV's IP address instead. In your router, give the TV a fixed IP so it never changes.

It says "Not connected"

Check the TV is on and both devices share the Wi-Fi. Turn the phone's mobile data off if the Wi-Fi has no internet. If it keeps failing, open ⚙ and tap Re-pair.

The TV asks for a code again

That happens if the TV was reset or its paired devices were cleared. Just type the new code shown on the TV.

How does the touchpad work?

Swipe to move, tap to select, and hold still for a long press (opens extra menus in many apps). The second dot under the touchpad switches to a classic arrow pad. OK and Back also support long press.

How do I turn the TV on?

Tap the power button. If "Wake TV when app opens" is on, opening the remote turns the TV on. If the TV is in deep standby, add its Wi-Fi MAC address in ⚙ (TV Settings › Network › About) so the remote can wake it. The TV's network standby setting must be on.

Can I use the phone's volume buttons?

Yes. While the remote is open and connected, the phone's volume buttons change the TV's volume.

How do I add the home screen widget?

Long-press an empty spot on your home screen, tap Widgets, find EK's Remote, and drag it out. It has power, volume down, mute and volume up. Pair in the app first.

How do I type or search?

Select a search box on the TV, then tap the keyboard button. Whatever you type appears on the TV straight away (capitals, symbols, corrections and all), and what's already in the TV's box shows in yours. Tap ↵ or your keyboard's search key to run the search. Close the box (✕ or Back) to get the TV's own keyboard back. The mic button opens the TV's search screen; voice input itself isn't supported.

The TV says to use the keyboard on my phone

That's normal while the type box is open: the TV hands its keyboard to your phone. Close the type box and the TV's own keyboard comes back.

How do I put a photo on the swipe pad?

Open ⚙ › Remote › Swipe pad photo › Choose, and pick a photo from your gallery. It's cropped to fill the pad (never stretched) and kept only on this phone; it isn't uploaded anywhere. Use Photo strength to make it fainter or stronger, or Remove photo to go back to plain.

How do updates work?

Changes to how the remote looks arrive by themselves the next time you open the app. When a new app version is out, a banner offers "Update": tap it and confirm Android's install screen. Your pairing and settings stay.

An app button opens the wrong thing

App buttons ask the TV to open that service's link. If the app isn't installed on the TV, or doesn't accept links, nothing happens or a browser opens. Install the app on the TV first.

How do I turn off vibration or change the theme?

Open ⚙ › Remote. Vibrate on touch, and Theme (Auto, Dark or Light) are there.
